Hertfordshire Council consults on disposing part of its art collection

The Council of Hertfordshire is planning to deaccession about 90% of its collection. Before this is executed, they are asking the public for advise.

Read more on the Hertfordshire website.

About the Author: Dieuwertje Wijsmuller

Dieuwertje Wijsmuller (Baarn, 1981) is a Dutch museologist, specialized in deaccessioning and disposal practices. With her company CreativeCultureConsultancy she supports museums, city councils and private (corporate) collections in collection management issues. She believes that processes such as deaccessioning and disposal should be executed on a transparent and responsible way and supports institutions in implementing such processes.

About this website

On this website, you will find the (legal) possibilities and latest news regarding this collections management tool. It may serve as inspiration for your country or institute if you want to develop or change your (existing) policies, or it can be used as research information.
